Before You Book

Common Questions

Are you taking new clients?

Yes. Book a consultation, or a consultation alongside your first appointment, so your stylist has time to talk properly before starting.

What happens at a first appointment?

A consultation at the mirror before anything begins — your hair's history, what you want, what is realistic today versus across a plan, and the price. Then the service itself.

How do I know what it will cost?

You are quoted before the service starts, not after. Colour and extensions vary with your hair's length, density and history, which is why the quote comes after your stylist has seen it.

Can I request a particular stylist?

Yes, and please do. Tell us when you book. If your usual stylist is fully booked we will tell you rather than quietly move you to someone else.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for notice so the time can go to someone else — colour and braiding appointments hold a lot of hours. The specific window is confirmed when you book.

Do you work with my hair texture?

Tell us your texture when you book and we will match you with the right stylist for it. That is a more useful answer than a blanket yes.

Do you take walk-ins?

It depends on the day and the service. Booking is safest for anything longer than a cut; call and ask about walk-ins for today.
