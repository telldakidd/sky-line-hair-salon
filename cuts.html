A consultation, then a cut built for your hair's texture and growth pattern — and for the ten minutes you will actually spend on it at home, rather than the forty the photo needed.

The consultation is the appointment

Most disappointing haircuts are agreed before a single section is taken. Bring pictures — of what you want and, just as usefully, of what you have hated. Your stylist will tell you what your hair will and will not do, because a cut that works on straight fine hair can behave completely differently on a dense curl pattern. It is a better conversation to have at the mirror than at the end.

Built around your routine

A shape that needs a round brush and twenty minutes is a great cut for someone who enjoys that and the wrong cut for someone who does not. Tell your stylist honestly what you do in the morning. The cut gets adjusted around that, and so does what we suggest you use — which is sometimes nothing new at all.

What is included

  • A proper consultation before anything is cut
  • Shampoo and finish
  • How to get the same result at home, shown not just described
  • A realistic interval before your next appointment

What to expect

  1. Consultation At the mirror, dry, before you are gowned. We look at your hair's growth, density and texture and agree the shape.
  2. Shampoo At the basin, with whatever suits your hair and scalp on the day.
  3. The cut Sectioned and cut to the agreed shape, checked as it dries because hair moves when it does.
  4. Finish Styled, and shown how to repeat it — including the version for the mornings you are in a hurry.

Common Questions

How often should I get it cut?

It depends on the shape and how fast your hair grows. Your stylist will suggest an interval for your cut specifically, and it is a suggestion, not a rule.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do, and bring more than one. Photos of what you do not want are just as useful. Your stylist will tell you honestly how close your hair can get to it.

Do you cut all hair types and textures?

Ask when you book and we will match you to the right stylist for your texture. That is a better answer than a blanket yes.
