Where your hair is starting from

What is achievable in one appointment depends on what is already on your hair. Box colour, previous lightening, henna and hard water all change how hair lifts, sometimes dramatically. Being straight with your stylist about your hair's history is the single most useful thing you can do — not to be told off, but because it changes the plan and the timing.

Big changes are staged on purpose

Going several shades lighter, or correcting a colour that went wrong, is usually more than one appointment. A stylist who tells you that is protecting your hair, not upselling you. Pushing it all into one session is how hair ends up damaged, and repairing that costs far more time than the staging would have.

How we work

  • A consultation and, where needed, a strand test before we start
  • A staged plan for big changes, with the timeline told upfront
  • A quote before the colour goes on, not after
  • Home care that keeps it, including the parts that cost nothing

What to expect

  1. Consultation Your hair's history, what you want, and what is realistically achievable in one sitting versus across a plan.
  2. Test A strand test where the change warrants one, and a skin test in advance where the colour requires it.
  3. Application Sectioned and applied, then processed and checked. Longer changes are exactly as long as they need to be.
  4. Toning and finish Toned to the agreed shade, conditioned, and finished — with a realistic date for your next appointment.

Common Questions

I used box colour. Should I say so?

Yes, always, and nobody will judge you for it. It genuinely changes how your hair will lift, and a stylist who does not know is working blind.

How long will the appointment take?

Colour appointments vary enormously — a root touch-up and a full lightening are different afternoons. You will be given a realistic time when you book.

Do I need a skin test?

For some colour services, yes, and it has to be done in advance rather than on the day. We will tell you when you book if yours needs one.
